# Tesria brand kit

The Tesria mark is a stack of four layers. Each layer is one thing Tesria does, and each has an
official color:

| Layer (top to bottom) | Meaning | On light backgrounds | On dark backgrounds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · **Write** (the diamond) | Writing pages, together | `#0c66e4` | `#6cb6f7` |
| 2 · **Keep** | Backups, restores, running it | `#1a6c45` | `#4bce97` |
| 3 · **Share** | Permissions, publishing, packs | `#5b47ba` | `#b8acf6` |
| 4 · **Automate** | API, webhooks, AI assistants (MCP) | `#9a4d00` | `#fea362` |

The colors are Tesria's own accent palette: blue, green, purple and orange. The light-background
shades are dark enough to read on white, and the dark-background shades are bright enough to read
on navy. Three pairs are exactly Tesria's accent values. For Write, the dark shade `#6cb6f7` is
the one Tesria already uses for its blue favicon, rather than the blue accent's `#579dff`.

## Files

| File | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `svg/tesria-mark.svg` | The favicon, and anywhere the background may be light or dark: it switches shades with the viewer's setting |
| `svg/tesria-mark-on-light.svg` | Light backgrounds |
| `svg/tesria-mark-on-dark.svg` | Dark backgrounds |
| `svg/tesria-mark-mono.svg` | One color (`currentColor`), where four colors can't be shown |
| `app-icon/light/` | The app icon for light backgrounds (deep shades): SVG, PNGs from 16 to 1024 px, and `favicon-light.ico` |
| `app-icon/dark/` | The app icon for dark backgrounds (bright shades): SVG, PNGs from 16 to 1024 px, and `favicon-dark.ico` |
| `colors.css`, `colors.json` | The four colors as tokens, with light and dark values |

## Geometry

The mark is drawn in a 24 × 24 box. The layers are open strokes with no fill, drawn bottom to top
so each layer sits above the one beneath it:

```svg
<svg viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round">
  <path d="M4 17.25l8 4 8-4"      stroke="…automate…"/>
  <path d="M4 13.75l8 4 8-4"      stroke="…share…"/>
  <path d="M4 10.25l8 4 8-4"      stroke="…keep…"/>
  <path d="M12 2.75l8 4-8 4-8-4z" stroke="…write…"/>
</svg>
```

- **Stroke width:** 2 at 32 px and below (favicons, small icons), 1.8 at larger sizes in the UI
  (tesria.com draws it at 28 px with 1.8).
- **Round caps and joins** always.
- The layers are 3.5 units apart; the diamond is 16 units wide and 8 tall.
- **Smallest size:** 16 px. Below that, use the mono version or the app icon.
- **Clear space:** at least 2 units (1/12 of the mark's size) on every side.

## Do and don't

- Do keep the four colors in this order: Write, Keep, Share, Automate, from top to bottom.
- Do use the light-background shades on light surfaces and the dark-background ones on dark
  surfaces. `tesria-mark.svg` does this by itself.
- Don't recolor the mark with a user's chosen accent. These colors are fixed, whatever accent
  someone picks.
- Don't fill the shapes, change the stroke weight within one mark, rotate, skew or add effects.
- Don't put the mark on a tile or in a rounded square: the app icon is the bare mark.

## The app icon

The app icon is the mark itself, the same one beside the Tesria name in the header: no tile,
no background. It comes in two sets, and each file is transparent:

- **Light** (`app-icon/light/`): the deep shades, for light backgrounds.
- **Dark** (`app-icon/dark/`): the bright shades, for dark backgrounds.

A PNG or `.ico` can't switch shades the way the SVG can, so pick the set for where it shows:

- **Favicon:** `svg/tesria-mark.svg`, which adapts by itself. For an `.ico` fallback, use
  `favicon-light.ico` for light browser tabs, which are the most common, or `favicon-dark.ico`.
- **`apple-touch-icon` (180 px):** `app-icon/dark/tesria-app-icon-dark-180.png`. iOS fills a
  transparent icon's background with black, so the dark set is the one that reads.
- **Web app manifest (192 and 512 px):** match the set to the app's background color.

## Updating Tesria

In the app, the mark is drawn in three places that have to change together:

1. `src/web/src/components/BrandMark.tsx`: the header mark. Four paths, each with its color.
2. `src/web/public/favicon.svg`: replace it with `svg/tesria-mark.svg`.
3. `faviconSvg()` in `src/web/src/theme.ts`: today it repaints the favicon in the user's accent.
   With official colors it can draw the four-color mark instead, or be removed so the static
   favicon stands. That's a product decision: keep an accent-colored favicon, or use the
   official colors everywhere.

Two things to keep as they are:
- **Instance branding:** an uploaded logo or favicon still replaces the mark. That's
  the instance's own brand, not Tesria's.
- **Exported sites:** these draw the mark too, so check the export template after updating the
  header.

The kit is generated by `scripts/brand-kit.mjs` in the tesria.com repository, from the same
geometry and colors the website uses.
