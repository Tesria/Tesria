# Governance

How decisions about Tesria are made, stated plainly so nobody has to guess.

- **One maintainer, for now.** Tesria has a single maintainer, who decides
  what goes in, reviews and merges changes, and makes releases.
- **Decisions in the open.** Proposals and the reasons for decisions are
  written down in issues and pull requests, and the direction of the
  project in `docs/dev-plan.md` and `docs/roadmap.md`.
- **Anyone may propose anything.** See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). A
  proposal may be turned down; when it is, the reason is given.
- **This will change as the project grows.** When others take on regular
  responsibility, they become maintainers, and this file will say how
  decisions are shared between them.
