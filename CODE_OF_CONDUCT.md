# Code of conduct

Tesria wants to be a project anyone can take part in: asking a question,
reporting a bug, or proposing a change. That only works if people are
treated well, so everyone taking part agrees to this.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new. Everyone was once.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Assume good intent, and say what you
  think plainly and politely.
- Take criticism of your work gracefully, and give it carefully.
- Own your mistakes, and help put them right.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning remarks about anyone, for any reason,
  including who they are or where they come from.
- Sexual language or attention, threats, and encouraging anyone to harm
  themselves.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Continuing something after being asked to stop.

## Where it applies

Everywhere the project is: its repository, issues and pull requests, its
discussions, and anywhere you represent it.

## Reporting a problem

Write to the address in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) with `[tesria conduct]`
in the subject, saying what happened and where. Reports are read by the
maintainer, kept confidential, and answered.

Depending on what happened, the response may be a private word, a public
warning, or a ban from the project's spaces, for a time or for good.
